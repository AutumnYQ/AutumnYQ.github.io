PhD Student  ·  Computing Science, Simon Fraser University

I am a PhD student in Computing Science at Simon Fraser University, advised by Prof. Wuyang Chen. My research interests lie in physics-informed 3D/4D reconstruction.

I received my MS degree from Beijing Forestry University. I worked at the Tsinghua Laboratory of Brain and Intelligence for one year and at Qiyuan Lab for two years, advised by Prof. Xiaolin Hu and Prof. Liang Tang.
